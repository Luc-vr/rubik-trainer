# CFOP-trainer

Learn and practice the CFOP algorithms for the 3x3 cube: all 41 F2L, 57 OLL and 21 PLL cases.

## Features

- **Case overview** – Browse every F2L, OLL and PLL case, grouped by shape or type. Filter down to the 2-look OLL and PLL cases if you're still learning the beginner route.
- **Case pages** – Each case shows how to recognize it, the main algorithm and alternatives, and a 3D cube that plays the algorithm. You can step through it move by move, with each move explained.
- **Recognition quiz** – Get shown a case from a random angle and pick its name out of four options. Cases you get wrong come back more often.
- **Timer per case** – Get a scramble that sets up one of the cases you picked, then time your solve. Keeps track of best times, ao5, ao12 and your slowest cases, with +2 and DNF penalties.
- **Notation guide** – Every move type (R, U', r, M, x …) with a 3D demo, plus the common triggers like the sexy move and Sune.
- **Progress saved locally** – Quiz results and times are stored in your browser. No account needed.
- **Settings** – Light, dark or system theme, and Dutch or English.
